Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Echoverge Post-Call Quality Analysis.

How do I upload a call recording?
Go to the Upload page from the sidebar, choose the agent and employee audio files and a transcript (if available), then click Upload. Supported audio formats: WAV, MP3.
Why don't I see my recently uploaded call on the dashboard?
Processing can take a few minutes depending on file size and system load. If it doesn't appear after several minutes, check upload logs and ensure the file formats are supported.
What do the sentiment and calm scores mean?
Sentiment reflects the emotional tone identified in the transcript (positive, neutral, negative). The Calm Score is an audio-derived metric indicating how calm the agent sounded. Use both to assess agent-customer interactions.
How is SOP adherence measured?
SOP adherence looks for key checklist items in the call (identity verification, security questions, etc.) and scores adherence as a percentage of expected steps followed.
Who can access the dashboard and reports?
Only authorized users can access the application. Authentication is handled via Microsoft sign-in; contact your administrator to grant access.
How do I get help or report an issue?
Use the Contact / Team page or email support@echoverge.com. Include the call ID and timestamp for faster troubleshooting.
